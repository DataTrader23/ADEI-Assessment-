# ADEI-Assessment-
Data science 
Problem Statement and Project Objectives
The main objective of this project is to analyse ADEI's customer support tickets to understand how effectively the support team handles and resolves customer queries.
The analysis will focus on the following areas:
1. Ticket Status and Resolution
Determine how many tickets have been successfully resolved and how many remain open or pending. This will help establish the team's overall workload and identify unresolved customer requests.
2. Communication Channel Performance
Analyse the different channels customers use to submit tickets, including WhatsApp, Email and Web. Compare how long tickets take to be resolved through each channel to identify which channel has the fastest resolution time and which performs poorly.
3. Channel Effectiveness
Determine which communication channel provides the best overall resolution performance. The aim is to understand whether customers receive faster support through WhatsApp, Email or Web and identify areas where the company could improve its support services.
4. Most Common Customer Issues
Identify which ticket categories occur most frequently. This will help ADEI understand the types of problems customers experience most often and where additional support or resources may be needed.
5. Category Resolution Performance
Compare resolution times across the different ticket categories to identify which types of customer issues take the longest to resolve and which are resolved the fastest.
Overall Goal
The overall goal is to use the available ticket data to identify patterns in customer support performance, highlight areas that require improvement and provide ADEI with data-driven recommendations for improving its customer service operations.
